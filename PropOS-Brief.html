How PropOS works

5 layers of personalisation.
No other tool does this.

Zenu sends a mail merge. PropOS generates a unique, financially-grounded message for every single contact — in the agent's own voice — in under 3 seconds per contact.

Layer 1 — CRM Intelligence

What the agent knows

AI extracts the most personal detail from free-text notes: "Karen mentioned wanting a bigger kitchen" becomes the hook that opens the message.

Layer 2 — Life-Stage Inference

What the notes imply

AI infers structured context from unstructured notes: kids' ages, school proximity concerns, property pain points, decision-maker. Haiku runs this at ~$0.001 per contact.

Layer 3 — Suburb Context

What the market says

Injects concrete local facts: "3 homes sold on your street in Q1 at $895K average." Grounds the message in data the contact can verify.

Layer 4 — Timing Triggers

Why now matters

CGT deadlines, EOFY, spring season, rate decisions, school transitions. Each message arrives at the moment the contact is most likely to act.

Layer 5 — Relationship Memory

What was said before

Every prior interaction is threaded and referenced. The Day 14 follow-up says "Last time you mentioned the garden was getting on top of you both" — because it does.

$399/mo Pro plan. Stripe Checkout + webhook. Payment wall on login. Trial period (14 days). ~2 days.

P1

POST /api/import-csv. Accepts Zenu/Rex/AgentBox exports. Maps columns automatically. Bulk upserts to past_buyers table.

"Mark as listing won" button on each contact. Feeds the ROI dashboard. This is the retention tool — agents who see their number never cancel.
