How it works

From dormant database to booked appraisals in three moves.

Every past buyer in your CRM is a future vendor. PropOS works the full loop, the way platforms like Realtair handle the pitch, but starting one step earlier, with AI prospecting.

01

Import & analyse

Drop in your AgentBox CSV or XLSX export. PropOS computes each contact's current equity, CGT position, and hold period, then segments them: sell-for-profit investors, downsizers, upsizers, ready-to-move owners.

88 contacts → 35 hot leads
02

Outreach in your voice

Hyper-personalised SMS and email for every lead, referencing what they bought, when, what it's worth now, and what their neighbour just sold for. Vetted copy, no robotic AI tells, your sign-off.

SMS + email, one click, all leads
03

Pitch & pounce

Send a branded, trackable Price Update with live comparable sales. The moment the vendor opens it, you get the alert and a personalised call script. Strike while the iron is hot.

Open alert → call script → listing

The flywheel

One lead, two pipelines.

Every buyer you've ever sold to is simultaneously a future vendor and live buyer demand. PropOS runs both sides from the same record.

1

Add a past vendor in 10 seconds

Name, suburb, rough purchase year. PropOS back-fills an estimated position and drops them into the pipeline as a re-engaged lead.

2

BuyerOS finds their next home

They get matched against your current listings by suburb, property type, and price proximity. "→ Recommend: 3 Fairholme Boulevard, Berwick" appears right in your CRM list.

3

VendorOS turns them into proof

When you pitch another vendor in that suburb, their Price Update carries a buyer-demand card: "Wei is actively looking for a 4-bedroom house in Berwick, exactly like yours." Demand sells listings.
